02 · THE OPERATIONAL CRISIS

The Four Failure Modes of the Pre-System Era

Manual workflows were capping business growth and leaking profits on every departure.

01

Booking & Pricing Friction

Bookings managed over phone calls and DMs. Quotes given from memory led to frequent child rate and holiday surge errors with double-booking risks.

02

The Profitability Blindspot

Trips ran fully booked yet lost money on diesel and market groceries. No per-trip cost tracking existed to identify margin leakage.

03

3-Day Month-End Agony

Reconciling monthly revenue against costs took 3 days of manual spreadsheet entry from paper receipts and WhatsApp logs.

04

Zero Internal Controls

Staff spending cash also approved their own expenses. Kitchen supplies, blankets, and boat spares vanished with no audit trail.

06 · INVENTORY INTEGRITY

Dual-Register Stock Engine: Eliminating Shrinkage

Permanent category tagging, condition lifecycle tracking, and automatic monthly carry-forward.

Permanent Item Register

Every asset receives an immutable category code (e.g. BHELA-KIT-0001, BHELA-ENG-0012). Tracks model, serial, purchase bill, warranty, and assigned custodian.

Condition Lifecycle

Assets audited across: Good → Repairable → Under Repair → Damaged → Scrapped. Distinguishes consumables from fixed capital assets.

Variance Reconciliation

Physical count vs. system count reconciled at month-end. Closing count becomes next month's opening count automatically. Variances require mandatory justification.
